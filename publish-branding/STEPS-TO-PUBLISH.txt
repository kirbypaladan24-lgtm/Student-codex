PART 3 - WHAT STUDENTS WILL SEE (nothing for you to do)
-------------------------------------------------------

- The FIRST time each student signs in with Google or imports from
  Classroom, Google shows an "unverified app" warning screen. This is
  normal for apps that use sensitive Classroom scopes without full
  Google verification. The student taps "Advanced", then "Go to
  Student Codex (unsafe)", and gets the normal consent screen.

- Unverified production apps are capped at 100 NEW users per day,
  which is far more than a class needs.

- Google may email you periodic "verification required" reminders.
  For a class-only app they are safe to ignore. Full verification
  would require the policy to be hosted on your own domain plus
  security review paperwork - not worth it for this use case.

- Grants that already exist (both of your accounts) keep working
  untouched.
