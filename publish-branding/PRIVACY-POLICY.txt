STUDENT CODEX - PRIVACY POLICY
Last updated: October 26, 2026

This privacy policy explains how Student Codex ("the app") handles your
information when you use it. Student Codex is a personal school companion
app for university students: it shows your Google Classroom coursework,
assignments, and deadlines in one clean view (the Beacon) together with
the courses you are enrolled in.

If you have any questions about this policy, contact us at
kirbypaladan24@gmail.com.


1. INFORMATION WE COLLECT

A. Account information
   - The email address and display name you provide when you create an
     account (email and password sign-up, or Google Sign-In).

B. Course and school details you enter
   - The courses you enroll in manually and any school details you fill
     in during sign-up or later in Settings.

C. Google Classroom data (only after YOU connect your account)
   - The names, sections, and teacher/roster information of the Google
     Classroom courses you choose to import
   - The coursework in those courses: titles, descriptions, due dates,
     and completion status
   - The Google account email you selected when importing, so that
     future syncs keep using the exact account you chose

   The app uses READ-ONLY Google Classroom permissions. It never posts,
   edits, submits, or deletes anything in your Google Classroom.

D. Authorization tokens
   - Short-lived Google access tokens used to read the data above
     while a sync is running.


2. HOW WE USE THE INFORMATION

- To display YOUR OWN coursework, deadlines, and classes inside the app
- To re-sync your coursework in the background so deadlines stay current
- To keep your account working across reinstalls and devices

We do NOT use your information for advertising, profiling, analytics, or
any purpose other than the features of the app itself.


3. WHERE DATA IS STORED AND HOW IT IS PROTECTED

- Your account, enrolled courses, and synced coursework are stored in
  the app's backend database (Supabase).
- Data is transmitted over HTTPS.
- Google access tokens are used only to call Google's Classroom API
  during a sync, and are never sold or shared with anyone.


4. SHARING WITH THIRD PARTIES

- We never sell, rent, or trade your personal information.
- The app contains no third-party advertising or tracking SDKs.
- Data is disclosed only when legally required.


5. YOUR CONTROLS

- Disconnect Google Classroom at any time from Settings. This stops all
  syncing immediately.
- Delete your account and all associated data by emailing
  kirbypaladan24@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my account".
  Deletion requests are processed within 30 days.


6. DATA RETENTION

Your account and coursework data are kept while your account exists, so
the app can keep showing them. Everything is permanently deleted when
you request account deletion, or when you delete courses from the app.


7. CHILDREN

Student Codex is intended for university students. It is not directed at
children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from children.


8. CHANGES TO THIS POLICY

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page
with a new "last updated" date.


9. CONTACT

Questions, concerns, or deletion requests: kirbypaladan24@gmail.com
