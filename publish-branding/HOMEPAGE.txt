STUDENT CODEX
Your school life, in one place.

Student Codex is a personal companion app for university students.
Connect your Google Classroom once and your courses, coursework, and
deadlines flow straight into your Beacon - one clean view of what is
due, what is coming, and what is past.


WHAT IT DOES

- Import courses straight from Google Classroom. You choose which
  Google account and which courses - it is never a guessing game.
- Automatic coursework sync. New assignments appear in the app without
  you lifting a finger.
- Deadlines that are actually upcoming. The Beacon counts what is due
  in the next 7 days so nothing sneaks up on you.
- Enroll in courses manually or pick them during sign-up.
- Sign in with email and password, or with your Google account.


PRIVACY FIRST

- Read-only access. Student Codex never modifies your Google Classroom.
- No ads, no trackers, no data selling.
- Disconnect Google Classroom anytime from Settings.
- See the Privacy Policy page for full details.
